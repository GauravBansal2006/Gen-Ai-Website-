## The demo is the product for five minutes

During a hackathon, your app may have a clever backend, a beautiful interface, and a dozen edge cases. The judges still experience it through a short story.

That story needs three things: the problem, the "aha" moment, and the proof that your team can execute.

## My pitch checklist

Before demo time, I now check:

- Can someone understand the problem in ten seconds?
- Does the demo show one complete user journey?
- Is the best feature visible without explanation?
- Do we have a backup recording?

## The underrated move

The strongest teams make the product feel inevitable. They do not list every feature. They show the one workflow that makes the judge think, "yes, this should exist."

```tsx
export function DemoRule() {
  return <p>Show the sharpest workflow, then stop talking.</p>;
}
```

### After the event

Write the postmortem while the memory is still fresh. The bugs, jokes, and near-disasters become useful if you capture them early.
